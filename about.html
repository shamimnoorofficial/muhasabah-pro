Frequently asked questions

What is Muhasabah Pro?

Muhasabah Pro is an Islamic Life Tracker: one free app for prayer times, Sehri and Iftar countdowns, Qibla, the Quran, Hadith, Masnun duas, zikr, Islamic habits, to-dos and a private journal for daily self-accountability (muhasabah).

Is Muhasabah Pro free?

Yes. Every feature is free. There are no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. You can support the app with an optional donation.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Muhasabah Pro is an installable app that works offline. Prayer times are calculated on your device.

Is my data private?

Your habits, records and backups stay on your device. Location is used only on the device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Read the Privacy Policy.

Which languages are supported?

English, Bengali (Bangla) and Arabic are hand-translated, and many more languages are available automatically.

মুহাসাবা প্রো কী?

মুহাসাবা প্রো একটি ইসলামিক লাইফ ট্র্যাকার — নামাজের সময়, সেহরি-ইফতার, কিবলা, কুরআন, হাদিস, দোয়া, জিকির, অভ্যাস ও ব্যক্তিগত ডায়েরি এক ফ্রি অ্যাপে।
