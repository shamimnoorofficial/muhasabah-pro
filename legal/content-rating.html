Content Rating: Everyone

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Muhasabah is suitable for all ages. It maps to IARC 3+, ESRB Everyone and PEGI 3: a personal habit tracker with nothing a child, parent or teacher needs to worry about.

Why it is safe for everyone

  • No objectionable content: no violence, sexual content, profanity, gambling, alcohol or drug references — in the app or its store listing.
  • No ads and no chat: no advertising, no messaging with strangers, no user-generated content shared with others.
  • Private by design: habits, journal entries and backups stay on the device; there is no public profile and no social feed.
  • No surprise costs: the core app is free; the optional Pro membership is bought only by an adult through a secure checkout.
  • Family friendly: habit names, reminders and journal prompts are self-reflection aids for daily Islamic practice.

For store review (IARC questionnaire)

  • Violence, sexual content, language, controlled substances, gambling: none.
  • User interaction / data sharing between users: none (single-user, on-device data).
  • Location: used only on-device for prayer-time calculation when the user allows it; never shared.
  • Ads: none. Personal data collected from children: none (anonymous on-device storage; see Privacy Policy).
