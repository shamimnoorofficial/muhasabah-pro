Refund Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

In short: Muhasabah: Islamic Lifestyle is completely free. There are no paid plans, subscriptions or in-app purchases right now, so there is nothing to cancel and you are never charged automatically. The only payments are voluntary donations.

1. No paid plans or subscriptions

Every feature of the app is free for everyone. We do not currently sell any membership, subscription or one-time upgrade, and the app does not take recurring payments. If you see a charge that you think came from Muhasabah: Islamic Lifestyle and it is not a donation you made, contact us and we will look into it.

2. Donations

  • Donations are voluntary one-time gifts to support the app. They are not normally refunded.
  • If you donated by mistake, donated twice, or were charged the wrong amount, contact us within 14 days of the donation and we will refund it.

3. How refunds are paid

Donations are processed by Stripe. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. They usually arrive within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

4. How to request a refund

Use the contact link below. Include the email address shown on your Stripe receipt, the date of the donation and the amount.

5. If paid plans are added later

If we ever add a paid option, we will update this policy before it goes on sale, so you know your refund and cancellation rights before you pay.
