Refund Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short: you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of any Pro payment. You can cancel Monthly Pro at any time. Lifetime members can also earn their full payment back through streak cashback.

1. 14-day money-back guarantee

If Muhasabah Pro is not right for you, contact us within 14 days of a payment and we will refund that payment in full. You do not need to give a reason. When a Lifetime payment is refunded, Pro access ends. Any streak cashback already paid is deducted from the refund.

2. Monthly Pro

  • You can turn off auto-pay at any time in the app (Get Pro → your membership → Auto-pay). You can also cancel from the billing page linked there.
  • Once you cancel, you will not be charged again, and Pro stays active until the end of the month you paid for.
  • Partly used months are not refunded after the 14-day window. The exception is a duplicate or mistaken charge, which we always refund.

3. Lifetime Premium and streak cashback

Separately from the guarantee above, Lifetime members can claim $1 back for every 40-day streak, up to 10 times, until the full price is returned. Cashback is a partial refund to your original payment method, and you keep Lifetime access.

4. Donations

Donations are voluntary gifts and are not normally refunded. If you donated by mistake or were charged the wrong amount, contact us within 14 days and we will put it right.

5. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Lemon Squeezy or Stripe. They usually arrive within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

6. How to request a refund

Use the contact link below and include the email address on your Pro account and the date of the payment. You can also reply to the receipt email from Lemon Squeezy, or use the Lemon Squeezy order lookup at app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders.
