Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Muhasabah Pro ("we", "the app") is a free Islamic life tracker for habits and daily self-accountability. We built it to keep your data on your own device. This policy explains what stays on your device, what reaches our servers, and why.

1. Data that stays on your device

Your habits, points, daily records, to-dos, notes, ledger, streaks, prayer-time settings, reminders and backups are stored in your browser on your device. We cannot see them. Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated on your device. If you allow location access, your location is used only on the device for that calculation and is not sent to us.

2. Data we store on our servers

  • Preferences: your language, theme and journal settings. These are linked to a random, anonymous cookie, not to your identity.
  • Journal: journal entries you write are saved so they are not lost. They are linked to a random, anonymous cookie on your device.
  • Daily reminder (optional): if you turn on push notifications, we store your browser's push address and keys, your time zone, your chosen reminder hour and language. This is not linked to your name or email. Turning reminders off removes it.
  • Account (optional): if you create an account, we store your email address and a securely hashed password (managed by Netlify Identity).
  • Account sync (optional): if you choose to keep your data in your account, a copy of your app data (habits, to-dos, ledger, journal and settings) is stored on our server under your account so you can sign in on another device. You can delete this copy at any time from the app.

3. No paid plans

All features are free. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases right now, so we do not collect billing details for any plan.

4. Donations

Voluntary donations are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never reach our servers. Stripe handles the receipt and payment records under its own privacy policy.

5. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you switch on Google Drive backup, the app saves a backup file in a private app folder in your own Google Drive. It uses the drive.appdata permission, so it cannot see your other files. The backup goes straight from your device to Google. We do not keep a copy.

6. Interface translation

For languages without a hand-written translation, the app's own button and menu texts are machine-translated once and cached. Your personal content is never sent for translation.

7. Third-party services

The app is hosted on Netlify. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, uses Google only if you connect Google Drive, and loads Stripe's script only when you open the donation form. Like any web service, these providers may see your IP address and browser details. We do not use advertising, tracking pixels or analytics profiling.

8. Cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies: anonymous preference and journal cookies, and a sign-in cookie if you use an account. Stripe may set its own cookies while you donate.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can export or delete the data on your device at any time from the app's backup settings, or by clearing the site's data in your browser.
  • You can delete your synced account data from the app, and ask us to access, correct or delete your account by contacting us. Donation records that the law requires to be kept are kept by Stripe.

10. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Children can use every feature without an account. Donations should be made only by an adult.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also announce it in the app.
