Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Muhasabah ("we", "the app") is a habit and self-accountability tracker. We built it to keep your data on your own device. This policy explains what stays on your device, what reaches our servers, and why.

1. Data that stays on your device

Your habits, points, daily records, streaks, prayer-time settings, reminders and backups are stored in your browser on your device. We cannot see them. Prayer times are calculated on your device. If you allow location access, your location is used only on the device for that calculation and is not sent to us.

2. Data we store on our servers

  • Preferences: your language and theme. These are linked to a random, anonymous cookie, not to your identity.
  • Journal: journal entries you write are saved so they are not lost. They are linked to a random, anonymous cookie on your device.
  • Pro account: if you create a Pro account, we store your email address, a securely hashed password (managed by Netlify Identity), and your membership status (plan, renewal date, cashback claimed).

3. Payments

Pro payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and merchant of record, or by Stripe. Donations are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to the payment provider and never reach our servers. From the provider we receive only what we need to activate your plan: order and subscription IDs, the email used, the plan, the payment status and renewal dates.

4. Google Drive backup (Pro, optional)

If you switch on Google Drive backup, the app saves a backup file in a private app folder in your own Google Drive. It uses the drive.appdata permission, so it cannot see your other files. The backup goes straight from your device to Google. We do not keep a copy.

5. Interface translation

For languages without a hand-written translation, the app's own button and menu texts are machine-translated once and cached. Your personal content is never sent for translation.

6. Third-party services

The app is hosted on Netlify. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and, when you pay, scripts from Lemon Squeezy or Stripe. Like any web service, these providers may see your IP address and browser details. We do not use advertising, tracking pixels or analytics profiling.

7. Cookies

We use a few strictly necessary cookies: anonymous preference and journal cookies, and the sign-in cookie for Pro accounts. Payment providers set their own cookies during checkout.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can export or delete the data on your device at any time from the app's backup settings, or by clearing the site's data in your browser.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your Pro account and membership records by contacting us. Payment records that the law requires us to keep are kept by the payment provider.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Children should use Pro payments only with a parent or guardian.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also announce it in the app.
